WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

what is unseen is not absence, but structure.

silence does not function as emptiness—it functions as pressure.

every line of dialogue carries an invisible counterline beneath it.

meaning forms in the space where articulation fails or refuses itself.

I am interested in narrative as residue—what remains after meaning has been partially erased.

silence as structure, not absence
dialogue as concealment mechanism
framing as psychological boundary
interiority shaping external motion
image as partial truth
story existing between cuts
I am drawn to the tension between what is visible and what refuses visibility.

My work treats narrative as something unstable—something that leaks, shifts, and reconfigures itself depending on what is withheld from it.

A character does not simply speak; they displace meaning through speech.

A frame does not contain reality; it interrupts it.

I am interested in the emotional logic of omission—how what is not said reorganizes everything that is.

I make films that exist slightly off from articulation.

Not incomplete—
but intentionally unresolved.
