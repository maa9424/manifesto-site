WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

what is unseen

silence is not empty

dialogue conceals

meaning lives between

I work in the in-between

silence is active
dialogue conceals
absence shapes meaning
what is unseen matters
interiority drives action
truth resists language
I am drawn to the visible and what remains hidden.

My work explores the space between seen and unseen.

Meaning is constructed through absence, silence, implication.

Dialogue communicates clearly conceals.

I create work where a surface story coexists with something deeper.

Something just beyond reach.

I inhabit the in-between.
